What this site does and does not do

MedicareBenefits.care is an independent publisher of public CMS data. It helps consumers, brokers, consultants, providers, payers, and analysts inspect Medicare Advantage plan filings — benefits, Summary of Benefits documents, formularies, pharmacy networks, and county-level availability. It is closer to a public-data reference than to a broker or carrier site.

CMS public dataReported, not recommendedNo broker influenceNo paid placementDirect from CMS sources

We do

  • Publish CMS plan, benefit, drug, pharmacy, Summary of Benefits, and county-level data
  • Normalize public Medicare Advantage datasets into plain, comparable tables
  • Link to official plan documents when the carrier publishes them
  • Provide neutral, metric-based comparison and filtering tools
  • Offer structured data exports for professionals
  • Explain Medicare Advantage terms in plain English

We do not

  • Sell insurance, or enroll anyone in a plan
  • Rank, recommend, score, or feature plans
  • Represent Medicare, CMS, any carrier, or any broker
  • Accept paid placement, or collect broker or sales leads
  • Guarantee provider-network, drug, or pharmacy accuracy
  • Replace Medicare.gov, SHIP, 1-800-MEDICARE, or confirmation with the plan
Before enrolling or making a coverage decision, confirm current details with the plan, Medicare.gov, your State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP), or 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227, TTY 1-877-486-2048). MedicareBenefits.care reports CMS-filed data and does not verify provider networks, pharmacy participation, or final plan terms.

Questions or corrections: info@medicarebenefits.care. See also our methodology and source ledger and glossary.

MedicareBenefits.care is an independent publisher of public Medicare Advantage plan data released by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). We do not sell insurance, are not a licensed insurance agency, do not represent any plan or carrier, and are not connected with or endorsed by the U.S. Government or the federal Medicare program. Benefits, premiums, and cost sharing are reported as published by CMS and may change; confirm details with the plan or at Medicare.gov (1-800-MEDICARE) before making any decision.
