      2026 Medicare Advantage landscape: what CMS posted

      CMS public files for contract year 2026 list 5,556 individual Medicare Advantage plan segments across 3,137 counties — 129,245 county-plan filings, 70,527 of them at a $0 consolidated premium. A filing is one plan segment available in one county; one plan segment is usually filed in many counties. Source: CMS files listed in the source ledger; site build September 26, 2026.
