3,405 of 5,556 plan segments indexed · data updated September 25, 2026

A Summary of Benefits (SoB) is the standardized document CMS requires every Medicare Advantage plan to publish for each contract year: the premium, deductibles, maximum out-of-pocket, and the cost sharing for each covered service and supplemental benefit, in a fixed order so plans can be compared. It is written by the plan, filed with CMS, and — together with the Evidence of Coverage — governs over any summary on this site.
