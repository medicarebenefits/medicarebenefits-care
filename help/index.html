Who to call: plan contacts, appeals, and free Medicare help

MedicareBenefits.care publishes plan data; it does not take calls, sell plans or represent anyone. These are the numbers the carriers and Medicare publish themselves, compiled September 26, 2026. The number on your member ID card always governs.

Free, independent help with any Medicare Advantage plan

1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227)
24 hours a day, 7 days a week; TTY 1-877-486-2048. Complaints, appeals help, plan problems, enrollment questions.

State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) — 1-877-839-2675
Free, unbiased one-on-one counseling in every state; shiphelp.org finds your local program.

Medicare Rights Center helpline — 1-800-333-4114
Free national counseling from an independent nonprofit.
