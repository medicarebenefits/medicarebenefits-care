Privacy

We do not ask for, store, or sell health or Medicare information. County search runs in your browser and sends nothing to us. A notification form, when offered, collects an email address only. Pro accounts use Firebase Authentication and Stripe for billing; we store your account ID and subscription status and nothing else. Third-party advertising networks, when present, may set cookies subject to their own policies.

We use Microsoft Clarity to see how visitors use the site (pages viewed, clicks, scrolling, and anonymized session replays). Clarity sets first-party cookies and is governed by the Microsoft Privacy Statement. It receives no name, email, or Medicare information from us, and the county search and email boxes are masked from recordings.

MedicareBenefits.care is an independent publisher of public Medicare Advantage plan data released by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS). We do not sell insurance, are not a licensed insurance agency, do not represent any plan or carrier, and are not connected with or endorsed by the U.S. Government or the federal Medicare program. Benefits, premiums, and cost sharing are reported as published by CMS and may change; confirm details with the plan or at Medicare.gov (1-800-MEDICARE) before making any decision.
