How to read this table

Yes — the plan's approved bid reports the benefit. No — the bid reports it is not offered. — — CMS did not publish a value for this field; that is not the same as No. Reported under an allowance column means the benefit is offered but CMS published no dollar amount. Premium is the CMS consolidated monthly premium (Part C + Part D). MOOP is the in-network maximum out-of-pocket. Star rating is the contract's overall rating in the CMS file used for this build. SNP plans (D-SNP, C-SNP, I-SNP) are limited to people who meet the plan's eligibility rules. A plan's own Summary of Benefits and Evidence of Coverage govern the details.
