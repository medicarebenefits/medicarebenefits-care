What Pro is for

  • Brokers and FMOs: pull every plan filed in your counties into a spreadsheet the day CMS posts — premiums, MOOP, deductibles, stars and 14 benefit flags in one row per plan — instead of paging through Plan Finder.
  • Plan and product teams: benchmark supplemental benefits and allowances against every competitor in a county; the year-over-year file (crosswalk-joined) shows exactly which plans changed premium, MOOP or benefits.
  • Provider groups and ACOs: map which carriers and plan types cover each county in your service area, and how many $0-premium and SNP products compete there.
  • Consultants, actuaries and analysts: clean, typed CSVs with stable column names across years; no HPMS access or PBP parsing required.
